09 / Watermarks

When is a window finished?

The engine guesses with a watermark: the newest event time seen, minus 5 minutes of allowed lateness.

A delivery from 13:25:19 arrives: W passes 13:15. 13:00–13:15 is published with 2. The third is still on a phone.

13:57:54: order 5043815’s delivery finally arrives. Its window closed half an hour ago: the event is dropped.

Watermark: “I have probably seen everything before W.” When W passes a window’s end, the window is published.
Late event: one that arrives after the watermark passed its window. It is dropped, or handled separately.
A build: two moments. First, only the deliveries that have arrived by 13:25:27. A delivery that happened at 13:25:19 arrives, so W = 13:25:19 − 5 minutes = 13:20:19. W has passed 13:15, the end of the first window, so the engine publishes 13:00–13:15 with 2 deliveries and forgets the window. Order 5043815’s delivery, which happened at 13:11, has not arrived yet. Press once more: now 13:57:54. The newest delivery seen happened at 13:50:30, so W = 13:45:30, and three windows have been published (2, 9 and 4 deliveries). Order 5043815’s delivery belongs to 13:00–13:15, which closed at 13:25: it is dropped, and the live count stays 2 instead of 3. With 46 minutes of allowed lateness it would have counted, but every window would also have been published 46 minutes later. Demo 3.3 section 6 traces the same rule; run it live.

11 / The words

Twelve words for streaming

Every one appeared on a slide today. Lab 3.3 and the next session use them all.

  • Stream · events processed as they arrive
  • Event · a record of one thing that happened
  • Log · an ordered list you only add to
  • Offset · a position in the log
  • Partition · one of the logs a topic is split into
  • Key · the field that picks the partition
  • Consumer group · readers with their own saved offsets
  • At-least-once · nothing lost; repeats possible
  • Idempotent · twice has the same effect as once
  • Event time · when it happened, not when we heard
  • Window · a slice of event time to count in
