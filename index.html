AI Engineering Track · Bootcamp

Modern Data Engineering

From raw sources to a tested, governed lakehouse: the bridge from a CS degree to the work a data team does every day.

AI Engineering Track 4 teaching days × 4h + capstone day 16 training hours ~50% concepts · ~50% hands-on Python and SQL

One case study, all week

Qafila, a fictional grocery delivery app in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam. Its messy sources feed one data platform, and every lab adds a piece of it. All data is synthetic and generated on your machine.

Store

The platform, DuckDB, window functions, Parquet

Ingest

APIs by hand, dlt, change feeds, cleaning with Polars

Model

Star schemas, dbt, data tests, orchestration with Dagster

Scale

Iceberg, Spark, streaming, governance under PDPL

Demonstrate

Team capstones: live demo, decisions, technical Q&A

Capstone

Teams of 2–3 build a working data platform, choosing one of:

  • Qafila Lakehouse: from raw sources to governed marts and an operations view
  • Open Data Platform: Saudi open datasets with tests and a small dashboard
  • Real-time Operations Monitor: replayed courier events, event-time KPIs and SLA alerts

Submit a public GitHub repository link before Day 5 with working code, a README, the grain of every fact table, and test results. Starter kit: labs/capstone/.

Rubric

Functional end-to-end pipeline30%
Architecture & data modeling decisions25%
Data quality & testing20%
Orchestration & governance15%
Presentation & reproducibility10%

Participants will be able to

  • Explain how a data platform fits together and choose between warehouse, lake and lakehouse
  • Write analytical SQL, including window functions, over databases and files
  • Build ingestion that is incremental and safe to re-run, from APIs and change feeds
  • Clean data, model it as a star schema and transform it with dbt under tests
  • Orchestrate pipelines and catch bad data before it reaches a report
  • Decide when Iceberg, Spark or streaming is worth it, and protect personal data under PDPL

Setup

Audience: fresh CS graduates, or anyone comfortable with Python and basic SQL (SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY). No data engineering experience needed.

Environment: Google Colab, or a laptop with uv. No Docker or cloud account. Lab 1.1 starts with 00_setup_check; see labs/README.md.

Stack: DuckDB · Parquet · dlt · Polars · dbt · Pandera · Dagster · Apache Iceberg · PySpark.
